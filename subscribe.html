Button not working? Subscribe manually

Copy this address into your calendar app’s Subscribe by URL option.

webcal://psychosocial-research-axis.github.io/rounds/calendars/rounds.ics
  • Google: Calendar → Other calendars → + → From URL.
  • Outlook: Calendar → Add calendar → Subscribe from web.
  • Apple: Paste the link into your browser and confirm the subscription.
  • Android/Yahoo/Other: Look for an option to subscribe to a calendar by URL. Paste the copied URL.
